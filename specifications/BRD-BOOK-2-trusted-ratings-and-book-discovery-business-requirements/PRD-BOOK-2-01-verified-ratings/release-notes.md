### Star ratings from buyers only, with averages on book pages and the catalogue

Star ratings now come only from customers with a completed, paid order for the book, with one rating per customer and book on a scale of 1 to 5 whole stars. The book page shows each book's average rating and how many ratings it rests on, and the catalogue list shows each book's average.

Customers can change their rating later while their order still counts, and rating the same book again updates the existing rating instead of adding another. Books with no ratings show **Not yet rated**. Comments stay visible to Quillmark only, and the storefront no longer offers deleting ratings.
